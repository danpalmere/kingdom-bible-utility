Step 1: The Interview

"Where does it hurt?" or "What would you like prayer for?" Ask simple, natural questions. Listen to the Spirit as they speak.

Step 2: The Diagnosis

Is this condition natural/physical, emotional, or spiritual? Ask God for wisdom.

Step 3: Prayer Strategy

Determine whether to pray a blessing, ask the Father, or speak with Kingdom authority over the pain in Jesus' name.

Step 4: Command / Prayer

Pray with eyes open. Keep it quiet and humble. Speak directly to the condition in the authority of Christ.

Step 5: Post-Prayer Direction

Ask: "What are you experiencing right now?" Reassure them of God’s love and encourage them to walk in identity.
